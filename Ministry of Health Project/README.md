## Project Overview
This project analyzes the Nutrition BMINDS Food Insecurity Survey and Stress dataset collected in Jordan. The dataset was designed to evaluate factors related to well-being, including food security, resilience, mental health, and stress mindset among the population. Data was collected from over 900 participants across different regions in Jordan.

The main goal of this analysis is to explore the relationships between food insecurity, psychological distress, resilience, and stress mindset, providing actionable insights to support health and social planning initiatives.

## Dataset Description
The dataset consists of multiple scales measuring key aspects of well-being:

1. **Food Insecurity Experience Scale (FIES)**  
   - Measures severity of food insecurity through eight questions.  
   - Scores categorize participants into: High, Marginal, Low, or Very Low Food Security.

2. **Resilience Scale (BRS)**  
   - Assesses the ability to bounce back from stress using six items on a 1-5 Likert scale.  
   - Scores range from Low, Normal, to High Resilience.

3. **Psychological Distress (K10 Scale)**  
   - Evaluates emotional distress via 10 items on a 1-5 scale.  
   - Total scores indicate Likely Well, Mild, Moderate, or Severe Distress.

4. **Stress Mindset Measure (SMM)**  
   - Measures beliefs about stress as enhancing or debilitating across health, learning, and performance domains.  
   - Eight items rated from 0-4; higher scores reflect a stress-is-enhancing mindset.

Additionally, demographic information such as age, gender, and income is included, enabling analysis of well-being across different population segments.

## Power BI Dashboard
The Power BI dashboard visualizes key insights from the dataset, providing interactive and intuitive analysis. The main features include:

- **Food Security Overview**  
  - Bar charts and pie charts showing the distribution of participants across FIES categories.  
  - Comparison of food security levels with demographic factors such as age and gender.

- **Resilience and Psychological Distress Analysis**  
  - Scatter plots linking resilience scores with psychological distress levels.  
  - Trendlines illustrate the potential protective effect of resilience on mental health.

- **Stress Mindset Insights**  
  - Visualizations highlighting participants' stress mindset distribution (enhancing vs. debilitating).  
  - Cross-analysis with psychological distress and resilience to identify patterns.

- **Correlation and Relationships**  
  - Heatmaps and scatter plots to explore relationships between food insecurity, resilience, stress mindset, and psychological distress.  
  - Filters allow for dynamic exploration by demographic variables.

- **Interactive Filtering and Drill-Down**  
  - Users can filter by region, gender, age group, or income level to examine subgroup patterns.  
  - Drill-down features enable detailed exploration of individual variables and their interactions.

## Purpose and Impact
This dashboard provides a clear visual understanding of how food insecurity and stress-related factors affect well-being in Jordan. It is intended for policymakers, health professionals, and social researchers to:

- Identify vulnerable population groups.  
- Understand the interplay between nutrition, resilience, stress mindset, and mental health.  
- Support targeted interventions and programs aimed at improving population well-being.
