## Overview

This project explores large-scale taxi and ride-hailing datasets from **New York City (NYC)** and **Chicago**, analyzing trip patterns, demand, and pricing for different types of services including traditional taxis and app-based services like Uber. The goal is to provide actionable insights into urban mobility, demand fluctuations, and fare dynamics through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## Datasets

### 1. NYC Taxi & Uber Data
- **Sources**: NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission (NYCTLC) and FiveThirtyEight GitHub repository.
- **Time Periods**:
  - Yellow and green taxi trips: April – September 2014
  - Uber trips: April – September 2014
- **Attributes**:
  - Pick-up and drop-off times and locations (latitude/longitude)
  - Trip distances, fares, payment types
  - Additional features for Uber trips: pick-up time and location
- **Additional Data**:
  - Hourly weather conditions (temperature, rainfall) to study their impact on taxi demand
- **Insights**:
  - Yellow taxis dominate Manhattan and Queens, green taxis serve outer boroughs.
  - Uber usage is concentrated around transport hubs and shopping areas.
  - Weather and time-of-day significantly influence taxi demand.

### 2. Chicago Taxi Data
- **Purpose**: Examining app-based ride-hailing vs. traditional taxis.
- **Attributes**:
  - Trip origin and destination zones
  - Trip distance and duration
  - Passenger count and fare information
- **Insights**:
  - Patterns of taxi and Uber demand over time
  - Identification of peak hours and high-demand zones
  - Impact of urban factors like traffic congestion and city zoning on ride demand and pricing

---

## Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides a **visual analytics platform** to explore NYC and Chicago taxi datasets. Key features include:

1. **Interactive Maps**
   - Heatmaps of pick-up and drop-off locations
   - Borough-level and city-wide visualization
   - Hour-of-day color-coded patterns to show peak activity

2. **Temporal Analysis**
   - Hourly, daily, and monthly trends of taxi and Uber trips
   - Visualization of demand shifts during different times of the day

3. **Demand vs. Weather Analysis**
   - Correlation between temperature, rainfall, and ride demand
   - Insights into how weather affects trip frequency

4. **Fare and Pricing Analysis**
   - Average fare per trip across zones and time
   - Quartile-based fare categorization to highlight high and low pricing areas
   - Clustering of zones for better price prediction insights

5. **Comparative Insights**
   - Comparison of traditional taxis vs. app-based ride-hailing services
   - Borough-wise and zone-wise trip distribution
   - Identification of high-demand and high-revenue areas

---

## Purpose

This dashboard provides city planners, taxi operators, and ride-hailing services with:

- **Demand Forecasting**: Anticipate areas with high trip demand
- **Resource Allocation**: Optimize taxi placement and availability
- **Pricing Insights**: Understand fare distribution and factors affecting prices
- **Policy Decision Support**: Evaluate congestion, service coverage, and urban mobility trends
